Privacy Policy

Sportify: Sport Nutrition · Last updated 23 November 2026

Sportify is a shop app for sports nutrition and kinesio tape with delivery across Uzbekistan. The store is run by Mamurjon Dilshodov (Sportify). This policy explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Phone number: to sign you in and to contact you about your orders.
  • Name: shown on your orders so the courier knows whom to hand them to.
  • Delivery addresses: street, district, entrance, floor, apartment and the map point you choose.
  • Location: only while the app is open and only when you choose to pin an address on the map. It is not tracked in the background.
  • Orders: items, delivery time, chosen payment method (cash, Payme or Click), comments and loyalty points.
  • Favorites and cart: the products you save.

What we do not collect

No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics SDKs, no contacts or photos. The app does not take card details; payment happens at delivery or in the Payme / Click apps.

Where it is stored

Your addresses and orders are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), which processes them on our behalf. Your sign-in, favorites, cart and club points are kept on your device. See Firebase privacy.

Who sees it

Only the Sportify store team, to prepare and deliver your orders. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else, except when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Orders may be kept as long as accounting law requires.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved addresses in Profile at any time. To delete your whole account, open Profile › Delete account: your sign-in, saved addresses and club points are deleted right away. Placed orders stay with the store as long as accounting law requires. You can also write to us (see Contact) and we delete your data within 30 days.

Children

Sportify is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
