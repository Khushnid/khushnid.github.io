Privacy Policy

Quran & Science · 5 October 2026

In short: Quran & Science keeps your language, look, Arabic text size and bookmarks on your iPhone. It has no account and no server of its own. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them. Nothing you read, search or save is uploaded, sold or shared.

What the app stores

Your language, appearance, Arabic text size, bookmarks, and small counters used to decide when an ad may appear are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. Pictures you have viewed are kept in the app's cache so they load faster; iOS can clear it at any time.

What the app downloads

Photographs, video and sound clips come from Wikimedia Commons, and the verse recitations from the Islamic Network CDN. When you open a fact, your iPhone requests those files directly from those servers, so they see your IP address and basic technical details, as any web server does. Quran & Science sends them no account, no advertising identifier and nothing else about you. Sources and “Open on Quran.com” links open in a Safari window inside the app; those sites have their own policies.

Sharing

A fact or a verse is shared only when you tap Share or Copy, through the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad when a fact opens, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch, served by Google AdMob and limited to a General audiences rating. To show them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use device identifiers and technical information such as your IP address, device type, approximate location and how you interact with ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. Quran & Science does not ask to track you across apps (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt), so ads are shown without the advertising identifier. Where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later in More › Privacy choices. The ad SDK never receives which facts you read, your bookmarks or anything you type. Premium removes all ads.

What is not collected

Quran & Science itself does not collect, transmit or sell personal data, has no analytics of its own and does not track you across apps or websites. Apart from the advertising and the file servers described above, it contains no third-party code that collects data.

Purchases

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription handled by Apple through the App Store. Quran & Science receives only whether Premium is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to payments.

Children

Quran & Science is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
