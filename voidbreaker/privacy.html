Void Breaker: Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Void Breaker is a game published by Khushnidjon Keldiboev. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

The app has no accounts and no sign-in. Your game progress (stardust, Hangar upgrades, best stages and scores) is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob and Yandex)

The game is free and shows ads: full-screen ads between runs, at least three minutes apart, and videos you choose to watch for a reward (revive, double stardust, new skill cards). The ads come from Google AdMob, or from the Yandex Advertising Network if your App Store country is Russia (if the app cannot read it, a time zone in Russia counts instead). To show and measure ads, these companies may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostics. Yandex's ad software includes its AppMetrica service, which handles this data for Yandex.

The app asks your permission before it uses the advertising identifier for tracking, and you can change that in iOS Settings. Where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states) the app also asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices on the game's title screen.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to count how the game is used (for example which language is chosen and whether a reward video was finished) and Firebase Crashlytics to report crashes, with device model, iOS version and the state of the app at the time. This data is not used to identify you. Where the law requires consent, nothing is sent until you agree, and refusing turns both off.

What we do not collect

The app sends no notifications, does not read your contacts, files, photos, camera or microphone, and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

Void Breaker is not directed at children under 13.
