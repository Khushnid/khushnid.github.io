Void Breaker: Support

Help with the iPhone game.

How do I play?

Drag with one thumb to move. Let go to stop, and your ship shoots. Clear the room, fly out through the door, pick a skill and keep going.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts, so deleting the app also deletes your stardust, Hangar upgrades and best scores, and progress does not move to a new iPhone.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the whole game is inside the app. Ads need a connection. If no reward video is available, you get the reward anyway.

Why do I see ads?

The game is free and ads pay for it. Full-screen ads appear between runs, at least three minutes apart. Reward videos only play when you choose them, for a revive, double stardust or new skill cards. There are no in-app purchases.

How do I change the language?

Open Language on the game's main menu. There are 12 languages.

How do I change my privacy choices?

If you were asked for consent, Privacy choices is on the game's main menu. Tracking permission is in the iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking. See the privacy policy.
