Gargantua: Black Hole 3D

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Gargantua: Black Hole 3D is a real-time black hole simulator for iPhone. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no subscription. This policy explains the only data the app touches and who else can see it.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from the app. Your settings — graphics quality, exposure, music volume, and the rest — are stored on your device only and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows occasional full-screen ads when you change the graphics quality in Settings. Those ads are delivered by Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, AdMob may collect:

  • advertising and device identifiers,
  • coarse location inferred from your IP address,
  • whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped.

Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and the AdMob policies.

Your choices

  • Tracking. On first opening Settings, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Decline and you still get ads — they are simply not personalised. You can change the answer at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). A Google-certified consent form appears the first time you open Settings. You can reopen it any time from Settings › Made with curiosity › Privacy & ad choices.
  • Deleting everything. Deleting the app removes every setting it stored. Nothing survives elsewhere, because nothing was sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new date above.
