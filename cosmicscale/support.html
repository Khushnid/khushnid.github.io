Cosmic Scale — Support

Answers to common questions about the iPhone app.

How do I unlock a simulation, the Beyond pack or Ultra graphics?

The pulsar simulation is free. Each other simulation opens for good after one short video. The Beyond pack and Ultra graphics open for 24 hours after a video. Cosmic Scale Pro opens everything at once and removes every ad. If no video can be shown (for example, you are offline), the simulation opens just that once.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Pro stays with your Apple Account, and the app turns it on by itself when you open it signed in to the same account. If it does not, open Settings (the gear) → Go Pro → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Cosmic Scale Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged again. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.

My iPhone gets warm, or the app feels slow

Open Settings (the gear) → Graphics quality and choose Battery saver or Balanced. The app also offers this by itself when the phone gets hot. Turning off “Rotate objects” helps too.

What is the satellite view of Earth?

Zoom in on Earth as far as the 3D view or Compare goes and keep zooming: the app continues in Apple Maps satellite imagery over the same spot. Zoom back out to return. It needs an internet connection; Apple Maps receives your device's IP address and the imagery request, nothing else (see the Privacy Policy).

Do photos and sounds work offline?

The scale, comparison, 3D views and simulations work offline. Photographs, space sounds and Earth's satellite view are loaded from the internet when you open them.

Where are the sizes from? Are they exact?

Sizes come from NASA/JPL fact sheets and published measurements. Supergiant stars and the largest galaxies are uncertain by tens of percent; for black holes the size shown is the event horizon. Nebulae, galaxies and superclusters are artistic renderings drawn at true size.

The app is in the wrong language

Settings (the gear) → Language. Cosmic Scale speaks 15 languages, including right-to-left Arabic.
