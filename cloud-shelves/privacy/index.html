Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23. This policy covers the Cloud Shelves iPhone app and this website. Cloud Shelves has no server of its own and no sign-up. Your files go only to your own Telegram account, and the ads come from Google AdMob.

Who we are

Cloud Shelves is an independent app made by Khushnidjon Keldiboev. It is not made or endorsed by Telegram and is not affiliated with it. Contact: xushnudbek321@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

  • The index of your library: file names, dates, shelves and, if place names are turned on, the name of the place.
  • Small previews of your files.
  • Files that are waiting to be sent. They stay until they are sent.
  • Files you open from Telegram. They are kept for up to a day and then removed.

When you sign out, you can choose to also remove the index and previews from the phone. Signing in with a different Telegram account removes them.

What goes to Telegram

  • To sign you in: your phone number, the login code and, if your account has one, your Telegram password. They go to Telegram; the app does not keep them.
  • The files you choose, sent as copies to one channel in your account that only you are in. The app makes or finds this channel only after you agree.
  • A caption on each file with the shelf, the month and, if place names are turned on, the place name, written as hashtags.
  • One small index file pinned in that channel, so your shelves can come back on another phone.

When you rename or remove a shelf, or move a file to another shelf, the app changes those captions. The app reads only its own channel.

Telegram cloud chats, this channel included, are not end-to-end encrypted. Telegram's own terms of service and privacy policy apply to everything stored in your Telegram account.

What goes to Apple

If place names are turned on, the location saved in a photo is sent to Apple to look up the name of the city. You can turn place names off in the app's Sending settings.

What goes to Google for ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your choice where the law requires it. For ads and for measuring them, Google receives:

  • the phone model and system version,
  • an identifier for this app on this phone,
  • a rough area, worked out from your internet address,
  • which ads were shown or tapped, and performance, diagnostic and crash data from the ads code.

Google gets none of your files, shelves or file names, and nothing from your Telegram account. The app does not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, so it never shows Apple's tracking prompt. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose how ads may use your data, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Google's own privacy policy applies to the data it receives.

What we receive

Only totals from Google: how many ads were shown and what they earned. We run no server and no analytics of our own, and we never see your files, your messages or your phone number.

The App Store privacy details

The app declares this data to Apple. "Advertising" and "analytics" here mean Google AdMob's ads and their measurement; we run no analytics of our own.

  • App functionality, linked to you: phone number, photos or videos, and other content you choose. They are sent to Telegram, as described above.
  • App functionality, advertising and analytics, linked to you: coarse location.
  • Advertising and analytics, linked to you: device ID, advertising data and product interaction.
  • Advertising and analytics, not linked to you: performance data and other diagnostic data.
  • Analytics, not linked to you: crash data.

None of this data is used to track you.

Your choices

  • Choose which files to send. Nothing is sent on its own.
  • Turn place names off in the Sending settings.
  • Change your ad choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland).
  • Sign out and choose to also remove the index from the phone.

This website

This website is a set of plain pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
