What is the Telegram file size limit?

On a free Telegram account, each file you send can be up to 2 GB; with Telegram Premium the limit is 4 GB. Telegram's FAQ gives these two numbers, and the limit applies to every file on its own, whether it is a photo, a video or a document.

The exact number in bytes

"2 GB" is a rounded figure. Telegram's developer documentation explains how apps upload a file: it is cut into parts of up to 512 KB each, and a free account may use at most 4000 parts for one file. A Premium account may use 8000.

4000 parts × 524,288 bytes = 2,097,152,000 bytes. That is the real ceiling for a free account. If a file is close to 2 GB, look at its exact size in bytes before you try to send it, because a rounded size in a file manager can hide a file that is just over.

What the limit does and does not cover

  • It is per file. Each photo, video or document is checked on its own. Sending ten files of 1.5 GB is fine; one file of 2.5 GB is not, on a free account.
  • Photos, videos and documents are treated the same. A video sent as a file still has to fit under 2 GB.
  • Captions have their own limit. A caption on a free account can be up to 1024 characters, and up to 4096 with Premium, according to Telegram's developer documentation.
  • Premium raises the per-file limit to 4 GB. Telegram's terms note that documents over 2 GB can only be viewed in the latest version of the Telegram apps, so someone on an older app may not be able to open them.

Videos are the files that go over

Most often, the file that goes over is a long video recorded at high resolution. You have three honest choices:

  1. Make the video smaller by converting it at a lower quality or resolution. See how to make a video smaller than 2 GB.
  2. Trim it, so the part you keep fits.
  3. Split it into several files, each under the limit. It works, but you then have several pieces to keep together.

When you send a video the normal way in Telegram's iPhone app, the app shows a Quality setting from Very Low to Very High and converts the video before sending. If you want to keep the original instead, send it as a file, and then the 2 GB limit applies to the original.

Speed on free accounts

The size limit is not the only difference between free and Premium. Telegram's developer documentation says the upload and download speed of free accounts can be limited, and that this only happens after a large amount of data, tens of gigabytes or more. Telegram does not publish exact speeds, so be wary of anyone who quotes one.

How Cloud Shelves helps

Cloud Shelves is built around the free account's limits. It sends one file at a time, and when Telegram asks it to slow down, it waits.
