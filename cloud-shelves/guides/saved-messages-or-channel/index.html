Saved Messages or your own channel: which is better for files?

For a few files now and then, Saved Messages is enough. For a library you want to keep apart from everything else you save, a channel that only you are in works better. Both are cloud chats with the same 2 GB per-file limit on a free account, so the choice is about order, not about space or quality.

Saved Messages

Telegram's developer documentation describes Saved Messages as simply the chat with yourself. Every account has it.

  • Always there. There is nothing to set up, and it syncs to all your devices like any cloud chat.
  • Everything ends up here. It is also where you forward messages, links and notes to yourself, so files mix with everything else.
  • Grouped by source. Messages you forward to Saved Messages can be shown grouped by the chat they came from.
  • Tags are a Premium feature. Telegram Premium can add tags to messages in Saved Messages to filter them. On a free account you have captions, hashtags and search.

A channel that only you are in

A channel is a feed that its owner posts to. If you make one, add nobody and give it no public link, you are its only member.

  • Kept apart. Its search and its shared media tabs show only what you put there.
  • A name and a description. You can say what belongs in it, and Pin a message such as a list of your hashtags.
  • More than one if you like. You could keep one channel for documents and another for family videos.
  • Within the free limits. Telegram's developer documentation sets how many channels and supergroups a free account can be in at 500, so one or two more make little difference.

In Telegram's iPhone app, a new channel starts from New Channel. When it asks for the channel type, choose the one without a public link, and add no members.

Side by side

  • Set-up: Saved Messages needs none; a channel takes a minute.
  • Mixed with other things: Saved Messages often is; a channel holds only what you put in it.
  • Encryption: the same for both. They are cloud chats and are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • File size: the same for both: up to 2 GB per file on a free account, 4 GB with Premium.
  • Captions: the same for both: up to 1024 characters on a free account.
  • Other people: neither is shown to other users, as long as you add nobody to the channel.

Which one to pick

Pick Saved Messages if you keep a few files and like having everything in one place. Pick a channel if you are building a library of documents, photos and videos and want it to stay tidy for years. Either way, send photos as files, caption everything with a few hashtags, and keep your originals.

How Cloud Shelves helps

Cloud Shelves uses a channel. After you agree, it makes one channel that only you are in, or finds the one it made before, so your Saved Messages stay yours. It sorts your files onto shelves on your phone, writes the shelf and the month into each caption, and pins a small index file in the channel so your shelves come back on a new phone.
