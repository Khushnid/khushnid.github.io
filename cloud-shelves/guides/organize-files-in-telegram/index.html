How to organise files in Telegram

To keep files organised in Telegram, put them in one chat meant only for files, give each one a caption with a few hashtags, and find them later with the chat's search and its shared media tabs. Telegram's Chat Folders group whole chats, not files, so inside a chat it is the captions that do the sorting.

  1. Choose one chat for your files.
  2. Write a caption with hashtags on every file you send.
  3. Find files with the chat's search or by tapping a hashtag.
  4. Browse by type in the chat's shared media tabs.

1. Choose one chat for your files

Mixing files into the chats where you talk to people makes them hard to find again. Pick one place:

  • Saved Messages, the chat with yourself that every account has, or
  • a channel that only you are in, which keeps your files apart from everything else you save.

Both work on a free account. Saved Messages or your own channel compares them.

2. Caption every file

A caption is the text sent with a photo, video or file. On a free account it can be up to 1024 characters, and up to 4096 with Telegram Premium. A good caption is short and consistent:

  • what it is, in a few words ("passport scan", "lease contract");
  • one or two hashtags for the topic, such as #documents or #family;
  • a hashtag for the time, such as the year and month.

Pick your hashtags once and reuse them. #receipt and #receipts are two different tags to search. If you forget a caption, Telegram lets you edit it after sending: the iPhone app shows Edit Caption when you edit a message with a file.

3. Search the chat and tap hashtags

Open the chat and use Search this chat to find words from your captions. Telegram's developer documentation says that tapping a hashtag in a chat searches that chat for the same hashtag, so a tag works like a filter: tap #documents and you get every file you tagged that way. Telegram's FAQ confirms that hashtags are supported.

4. Browse the shared media tabs

Open the chat's profile to see its shared media. In the iPhone app the tabs include Media, Files, Links, Voice, Audio and GIFs. Photos and videos sent normally appear under Media; documents appear under Files.

Telegram's global search also has filters, such as Media, Links, Files, Music and Voice, when you are looking across all your chats.

Small habits that help

  • Send photos as files when you want the original quality. See how to send without compression.
  • Pin the one message you need most often, such as a list of the hashtags you use.
  • Write a channel description if you use a channel, so you remember what belongs there.
  • Do not rely on Premium tags. Saved Messages tags are a Telegram Premium feature, according to Telegram's developer documentation. Hashtags work on every account.

How Cloud Shelves helps

Cloud Shelves does the sorting on your phone. You put files on shelves such as Documents, Study, Family, Work and Videos, or your own, and the app shows them by shelf and by month with search by name. It also writes the shelf and the month into every caption as hashtags, so the same files stay easy to find in Telegram's own apps. When you rename a shelf or move a file, the captions are updated.
