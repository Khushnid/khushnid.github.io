Are Telegram cloud chats end-to-end encrypted?

No. Telegram's ordinary chats, which Telegram calls cloud chats, are not end-to-end encrypted. Telegram's FAQ says they use server-client encryption and are stored encrypted in the Telegram cloud. Only secret chats are end-to-end encrypted, and a secret chat exists only on the device where it was started.

Two kinds of chat

Telegram has two kinds of chat, and they work differently.

Cloud chats

  • This is almost everything: normal one-on-one chats, groups, channels and Saved Messages.
  • Messages and files are stored in the Telegram cloud, encrypted between your device and Telegram's servers and stored encrypted there.
  • Because they live in the cloud, they sync across all your devices. You can sign in on a new phone or a computer and see them.
  • They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Secret chats

  • Telegram's FAQ says all secret chats use end-to-end encryption.
  • Telegram's developer documentation describes secret chats as one-on-one chats between two devices. There are no secret groups or secret channels.
  • They are not part of the Telegram cloud and can only be opened on the device where they were started. If you sign in on another device, a secret chat is not there.
  • In the iPhone app you start one with New Secret Chat. The app describes them as end-to-end encrypted, leaving no trace on Telegram's servers, with a self-destruct timer, and without forwarding.

What this means for files you keep in Telegram

If you keep files in Telegram, they are in a cloud chat: a channel, a group or Saved Messages. That is exactly what lets you open them on every device, and it is also why they are not end-to-end encrypted.

Secret chats cannot fill that role. They are tied to one device, they do not sync, and they are meant for conversations between two people, not for a library of files.

So the honest summary is:

  • Files in Telegram cloud chats are encrypted on the way to Telegram and stored encrypted, but they are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Telegram's terms of service and privacy policy apply to them.
  • Files in a secret chat are end-to-end encrypted, but only on that one device.

Sensible habits

  • Think about what you keep in a cloud chat. Something you would not want stored by a messaging service is better kept elsewhere.
  • Keep your originals. A copy in Telegram is handy, but it should not be the only one.
  • Be wary of any app or website that calls Telegram storage end-to-end encrypted. For cloud chats, Telegram itself does not say so.

How Cloud Shelves helps

Cloud Shelves keeps your files in a channel that only you are in, and a channel is a cloud chat, so your library in Telegram is not end-to-end encrypted either. The app says this plainly in its privacy screen. What it adds is on your phone: the shelves and the index are built there, there is no server of ours in between, and Google, which shows the app's ads, gets none of your files.
