Telegram as cloud storage: the honest pros and cons

Yes, you can keep files in Telegram: what you send to its cloud chats is stored in the Telegram cloud and is there on every device where you sign in. But Telegram is a messaging service, not a storage product, so it is worth knowing the trade-offs before you rely on it for files that matter.

What works well

  • Large files. Each file can be up to 2 GB on a free account and 4 GB with Telegram Premium, according to Telegram's FAQ.
  • Every device. Cloud chats sync across your devices, so a file you send from your phone is there on your computer too.
  • Space on your phone. Telegram's iPhone app has a Keep Media setting. Media from cloud chats that you have not opened for that period is removed from the phone, and the app says it stays in the Telegram cloud and can be downloaded again.
  • Search. Telegram supports hashtags, and every chat can be searched. See how to organise files in Telegram.
  • No extra account. The files sit in the Telegram account you already use.

What to watch out for

Cloud chats are not end-to-end encrypted

Telegram's FAQ explains that cloud chats use server-client encryption and are stored encrypted in the Telegram cloud. Only secret chats are end-to-end encrypted, and they exist only on the device where they were started, so they are no use as a place to keep files. Read more in Are Telegram cloud chats end-to-end encrypted?

Your account can be deleted if you are away

Telegram's FAQ says that if you do not come online for a set period, 18 months by default, your account is deleted together with all its messages and media. You can change that period in the app's settings. If Telegram is where your files live, sign in from time to time, and check that setting.

Your files depend on your account

Telegram's terms of service say that breaking them can lead to an account being blocked for a time or for good. Your files live inside that account. Use your account the normal way and keep copies of anything you cannot lose.

Photos are compressed unless you send files

Telegram's own iPhone app describes sending from the File section as the way to share uncompressed media from your camera roll. A photo sent the normal way is not the original file. See how to send photos and videos without compression.

Speed on free accounts

Telegram's developer documentation says the upload and download speed of free accounts can be limited after a large amount of data, tens of gigabytes or more. Telegram does not publish exact speeds.

It is not organised like a drive

Telegram's Chat Folders group chats, not files. Inside a chat, files sit in the order you sent them. Captions, hashtags and search are what keep them findable.

A sensible way to do it

  1. Use one place just for files: Saved Messages or a channel that only you are in. See Saved Messages or your own channel.
  2. Send photos and videos as files, so they keep their original quality.
  3. Write a caption with a few hashtags on every file.
  4. Keep your originals on your phone, your computer or another service as well.
  5. Sign in to Telegram from time to time so the account stays active.

How Cloud Shelves helps
