Common questions

Is Cloud Shelves made by Telegram?

No. Cloud Shelves is an independent app. It uses the Telegram API with your own account, and it is not made, endorsed or supported by Telegram.

Do I need Telegram Premium?

No. Cloud Shelves works with a free Telegram account and does not use any Premium feature. On a free account each file can be up to 2 GB.

What does the app make in my Telegram account?

One channel for your files that only you are in. The app asks before it makes it, once, before you sign in. When you sign in again later, it finds the same channel. It reads only that channel.

Are my files end-to-end encrypted?

No. Telegram cloud chats, this channel included, use server-client encryption and are stored encrypted in the Telegram cloud, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. Keep your originals.

Does it copy my whole camera roll?

No. Only the files you pick are sent, one at a time. They are copies: nothing on your phone is moved or deleted.

Why are photos sent as files?

So Telegram keeps the original quality. Telegram shows them as files rather than as photos. You can change this in the app's Sending settings.

What happens to a video larger than 2 GB?

The app can make a smaller copy that fits under the limit, aiming at about 1.9 GB, and send that. It never cuts a video into parts, and your original stays on your phone.

Why did sending pause?

When Telegram asks apps to slow down, Cloud Shelves waits and then carries on by itself. Telegram's developer documentation says free accounts can have their upload and download speed limited after moving a lot of data.

I have a new phone. How do I get my shelves back?

Install the app and sign in with the same Telegram account. The app reads the small index file pinned in your channel and brings your shelves back.

Why are there ads, and can I turn them off?

Ads from Google AdMob pay for the app. There is a banner at the end of the Shelves screen and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you add files, never on your first day. Watching one short video in Settings turns ads off for 24 hours. There are no purchases.

What does the app keep on my phone?

An index of your library (names, dates and places), small previews, files waiting to be sent, and files you opened in the last day. The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I remove the app's data?

Sign out in the app and choose to also remove the index from this phone. The files you sent stay in your Telegram account until you delete them there.
