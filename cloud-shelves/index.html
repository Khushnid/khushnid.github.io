Your files, sorted on shelves, kept in your own Telegram account

Cloud Shelves is an iPhone app that uses your own Telegram account as a file library. You pick photos, videos and documents, the app sends copies of them to one channel in your account, and on your phone it shows them sorted on shelves.

What it does

  • Shelves. Start with Documents, Study, Family, Work and Videos, and add your own, each with an icon and a colour.
  • Months. Inside a shelf, files are grouped by month. Search finds them by name, shelf or month.
  • Scan to PDF. Scan paper documents with the camera and keep them as a PDF.
  • Already sent. Files you have sent before are marked, so you do not send them twice.
  • Videos over 2 GB. A free Telegram account takes files up to 2 GB each. A video that is too large can be made smaller to fit, instead of being cut into parts.
  • A new phone. Sign in again and your shelves come back from the channel.

How it works

  1. Before you sign in, the app asks once whether it may make a channel for your files in your Telegram account.
  2. You sign in with Telegram. The app finds that channel, or makes it: one channel that only you are in.
  3. You choose files and a shelf. The app sends copies one at a time. Photos go as files, so Telegram keeps the original quality.
  4. The shelf and the month are written into each caption as hashtags, so your files stay easy to find in Telegram's own apps too.
  5. A small index file pinned in the channel lets the app bring your shelves back on another phone.

Honest limits

  • Telegram cloud chats, this channel included, are not end-to-end encrypted. They use server-client encryption and are stored encrypted in the Telegram cloud. Keep your originals. More about encryption.
  • One file can be up to 2 GB on a free account. Cloud Shelves works with free accounts and never needs Telegram Premium.
  • Telegram's developer documentation says free accounts can have their upload and download speed limited after moving a lot of data. When Telegram asks the app to slow down, it waits and then carries on.
  • Uploads start only when you add files. The app does not copy your camera roll on its own.
  • The app sends copies. It never moves or deletes the files on your phone.
  • Telegram's terms of service and privacy policy apply to everything in your account.

How it is paid for

Cloud Shelves is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the end of the Shelves screen and, at most twice a day, a full-screen ad after you add files, never on your first day. One short video in Settings turns ads off for 24 hours. There are no purchases. The Privacy Policy lists what Google receives.

Cloud Shelves is an independent app. It is not made or endorsed by Telegram.
